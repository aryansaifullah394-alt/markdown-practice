### How to Play Football

1. Gather a team of 11 players
2. Set up goals on both ends of the field
3. Start the match with a kickoff
4. Pass, dribble, and shoot the ball to score
5. Follow the referee's rules

### Essential Equipment

- Football (soccer ball)
- Jerseys and shorts
- Shin guards
- Boots/cleats
- Goalposts
